## 1. Purpose

This standard defines layout, attribution, typography, and template rules for official letterheads, memoranda, presentation slide decks, operational workbooks, and corporate email signatures across **Irvantri Standards** (`Irvantri Standards Pte. Ltd.`). It ensures daily institutional correspondence, external presentations, and structured spreadsheets maintain consistent brand identity and legal attribution.

## 2. Scope & Applicability

### 2.1 In Scope
This standard applies to:
* All official corporate letterheads, formal letters, and internal or external memoranda issued by **Irvantri Standards** (`Irvantri Standards Pte. Ltd.`).
* All external and internal presentation slide deck masters, pitch decks, and briefing decks.
* All manually authored operational spreadsheets and structured workbooks—such as Google Sheets templates—used for registers, trackers, and schedules.
* All corporate email signatures used on official **Irvantri Standards** accounts.
* All executable correspondence, presentation, and workbook templates (`TPL-E04`) registered in `Library-Index.md` and Section 7 of this standard.

### 2.2 Out of Scope
This standard does not govern:
* Multi-page formal report covers, metadata panels, or running headers and footers (governed by `E01`).
* Non-approved document banners, watermarks, or archive filename suffixes (governed by `E02`).
* Commercial press bleed, ink limits, or vendor color proofing procedures (governed by `E03`).
* System-generated Comma-Separated Values (`CSV`) exports and automated data feeds produced by software systems (governed by `D05`).
* Corporate website footers, navigation bars, or digital product interfaces (governed by `D02` and `D03`).

## 4. Terms & Definitions

For terms shared across the library, refer to `Library-Index.md`, `C02`, `A02`, `A03`, `B01`, `B02`, `B03`, `B04`, `C01`, `C04`, `E01`, `E02`, and `E03`. For the purposes of this standard, the following definitions apply:
* **Corporate Email Signature:** A standardized text or `HTML` closing block appended to official email correspondence displaying sender identity, entity attribution, and contact details.
* **Letterhead:** A pre-formatted stationery layout carrying official brand marks, entity details, and margin geometry for formal letters and notices.
* **Operational Workbook:** A manually authored spreadsheet file—such as a Google Sheets template—structured with a controlled metadata tab and standardized data tabs for registers, trackers, or schedules.
* **Slide Deck Master:** A controlled presentation template defining aspect ratio, grid alignment, typography hierarchy, and cover or closing attribution slides.

## 5. Requirements

### 5.1 Official Letterheads & Memoranda
**5.1.1** Official letterheads and formal memoranda SHALL use A4 (`210 mm × 297 mm`) trim dimensions (`E03 §5.1.1`) and align margins to the `4 mm` Base Unit (`B04 §5.1.2`).

**5.1.2** Letterheads SHALL place the Parent-Endorsed Lockup (`B01 §5.2.1`) within `16 mm` Safe Margins (`E03 §5.1.2`) and maintain `0.25×` Primary Symbol width Clear Space (`B01 §5.3.1`).

**5.1.3** Formal correspondence SHALL display `Irvantri Standards Pte. Ltd.` (`A03 §5.2.3`), `A member of Irvantri Group` (`A03 §5.3.3`), and International Organization for Standardization (`ISO`) 8601 dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`) (`C04 §5.1.1`).

**5.1.4** Formal letters and memoranda SHALL use United States English (`en-US`) (`C01 §5.6.1`), SHALL NOT use contractions (`C01 §5.2.1`), and SHALL NOT bind `Irvantri Group Pte. Ltd.` (`A03 §5.2.4`).

**5.1.5** Printed single-sheet stationery SHALL use at least `120 g/m²` uncoated archival paper stock (`E03 §5.2.1`) and `B02` `ISO` Coated v2 color builds (`E03 §5.3.1`).

**5.1.6** Official letterheads and memoranda distributed electronically as `PDF` files SHALL conform to the `E01 §5.4` Digital Distribution `PDF` Profile.

### 5.2 Presentation Slide Deck Masters
**5.2.1** Official presentation slide decks SHALL use a `16:9` widescreen aspect ratio (`1920 px × 1080 px`) aligned to the `4 px` digital Base Unit (`B04 §5.1.1`, `C04 §5.3.2`).

**5.2.2** Presentation cover or closing slides SHALL display the Parent-Endorsed Lockup (`B01 §5.2.1`) and `A member of Irvantri Group` (`A03 §5.3.3`). Closing slides SHALL include `© [YYYY] Irvantri Standards Pte. Ltd. All rights reserved.` (`A03 §5.6.1`).

**5.2.3** Slide titles SHALL use the Display Typeface (`B03 §5.1.1`) and body copy SHALL use the Text Typeface (`B03 §5.1.1`) at `16 pt` minimum (`C04 §5.3.2`). Slide text SHALL meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (`WCAG`) 2.2 Level AA contrast (`B02 §5.5.1`).

**5.2.4** Slide layouts SHOULD follow the `75%` neutral, `20%` blue/navy, and `5%` **Signal Red** (`#DE0029`) balance (`B02 §5.4.1`). External presentations SHALL NOT display internal project codenames (`A03 §5.4.2`, `C02 §5.3.3`).

**5.2.5** Non-Approved presentation decks distributed externally for review SHALL display their controlled `C02 §5.4.1` status and version on the cover slide (`E02 §5.1.1`, `§5.2.1`).

**5.2.6** Slide decks on the `1920 px × 1080 px` canvas (`5.2.1`) SHALL apply the type scale below, published as `AST-D01-01` tokens (`D01`). At `96 px` per inch, `16 pt` equals `21.33 px`, so `24 px` is the smallest size on the `4 px` Base Unit that meets the `5.2.3` minimum. No slide text SHALL be set below `24 px`.

| Slide Role | Typeface | Size | Weight | Line Height | Token |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Slide title | Display Typeface (Noto Sans Display) | `64 px` | `800` | `1.2` | `primitive.font.size.slide-title` |
| Slide heading | Display Typeface (Noto Sans Display) | `40 px` | `700` | `1.2` | `primitive.font.size.slide-heading` |
| Slide body and captions | Text Typeface (Noto Sans) | `24 px` | `400` | `1.5` | `primitive.font.size.slide-body` |

### 5.3 Corporate Email Signatures
**5.3.1** Corporate email signatures SHALL use `HTML` or plain text with system sans-serif fallback fonts (`B03 §5.1.4`) and SHALL NOT replace the full signature with a raster image (`B01 §5.1.2`).

**5.3.2** Every corporate email signature SHALL follow the ordered block below, preserving `Irvantri Standards Pte. Ltd.` (`A03 §5.2.3`) and `A member of Irvantri Group` (`A03 §5.3.3`):

```text
[Full Name] | [Role Title]
Direct: +[Country Code] [Employee Number]
[Official Logo]
Irvantri Standards Pte. Ltd.
A member of Irvantri Group
[Official URL] | Office: +[Country Code] [Office Line]
```

> **Informative Note:** Omit the `Direct:` line when no employee-assigned corporate number exists, omit `| Office: +[Country Code] [Office Line]` when no main office line applies, and omit `[Official Logo]` in plain-text email clients (`C04 §5.4.1`).

**5.3.3** Where `[Official Logo]` appears in an `HTML` signature, it SHALL use a lossless `PNG` exported from an `AST-B01` `SVG` master with `0.25×` Clear Space (`B01 §5.1.2`, `§5.3.1`). The image SHALL use the Primary Master Lockup (`140 px` minimum) or Primary Symbol (`28 px × 28 px` minimum) with `alt="Irvantri Standards"` (`B01 §5.3.2`). Email signatures SHALL NOT embed the Parent-Endorsed Lockup image (`B01 §5.2.3`).

**5.3.4** Corporate email signatures SHALL omit unused telephone lines and trailing delimiters (`C04 §5.4.1`) and SHALL NOT use unofficial telephone abbreviations (`C02 §5.2.4`). Signatures SHALL NOT include personal phone numbers, personal quotes, promotional superlatives (`C01 §5.1.2`), unauthorized endorsement variants (`A03 §5.3.2`), or unauthorized badges (`A03 §5.5.4`).

### 5.4 Executable Templates (`TPL-E04`) & Corporate Boilerplates
**5.4.1** Shared letterhead, memorandum, and slide deck master files SHALL be registered as `TPL-E04` Executable Templates in `Library-Index.md` and Section 7, pinned to `E04 (vMAJOR.MINOR)` (`A01 §5.7.4`, `A02 §5.7.2`).

**5.4.2** External proposals and correspondence containing standardized company descriptions SHALL reproduce an approved `C01 §5.5.1` boilerplate verbatim without altering corporate names (`A03 §5.2.1`).

### 5.5 Operational Spreadsheets & Workbooks
**5.5.1** Manually authored operational workbooks SHALL include a leading `Overview` tab recording title, version (`vMAJOR.MINOR.ERRATA`), `C02` status, Owner, and last updated date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) (`A01 §5.5.1`, `C04 §5.1.1`). The `Overview` tab SHALL display `Irvantri Standards Pte. Ltd.` and `A member of Irvantri Group` (`A03 §5.2.3`, `§5.3.3`).

**5.5.2** Data tabs in operational workbooks SHALL use the Text Typeface (`B03 §5.1.1`) with tabular numerals (`B03 §5.3.1`) and freeze header rows. Table headers SHALL use `B02` palette tokens meeting `WCAG` 2.2 Level AA contrast (`B02 §5.5.1`). Codes and version strings SHOULD use the Monospace Typeface (`B03 §5.1.2`).

**5.5.3** Data tabs SHALL declare shared units in column headers (`C04 §5.4.2`) and format calendar dates as `YYYY-MM-DD` (`C04 §5.1.1`). Empty cells SHALL display `None` or `—` (`C04 §5.4.1`), and status columns SHALL pair exact `C02 §5.4.1` strings with `B02 §5.3.1` semantic colors.

**5.5.4** Shared operational workbook templates SHALL be registered as `TPL-E04` Executable Templates in `Library-Index.md` and Section 7, pinned to `E04 (vMAJOR.MINOR)` (`A01 §5.7.4`, `A02 §5.4.6`).

## 6. Conformance & Verification

A letterhead, memorandum, presentation deck, operational workbook, email signature, or template conforms to `E04` if and only if all of the following objective checks pass:

| Check ID | Verification Criterion | Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `E04-C1` | Letterheads and memoranda use A4 / `4 mm` geometry (`5.1.1`), `B01` lockup within `16 mm` Safe Margins (`5.1.2`), required `A03`/`ISO` 8601 attribution (`5.1.3`), formal `en-US` copy without contractions or parent-entity binding (`5.1.4`), `120 g/m²` archival stock when printed (`5.1.5`), and `E01 §5.4` digital `PDF` rules when distributed electronically (`5.1.6`). | Correspondence & channel audit |
| `E04-C2` | Slide decks use `16:9` (`1920 px × 1080 px`) aligned to `4 px` (`5.2.1`), display `B01`/`A03` cover and closing attribution (`5.2.2`), use the `B03` Display Typeface and `16 pt` minimum Text Typeface meeting `WCAG` 2.2 Level AA contrast (`5.2.3`), omit external codenames (`5.2.4`), mark non-Approved review decks (`5.2.5`), and apply the `64 px` / `40 px` / `24 px` slide type scale with no slide text below `24 px` (`5.2.6`). | Slide master & deck audit |
| `E04-C3` | Corporate email signatures use `HTML` or plain text with sans-serif fallbacks without full-signature raster images (`5.3.1`), follow the mandatory ordered block (`5.3.2`), use an `AST-B01`-derived Primary Master Lockup or Primary Symbol `PNG` without embedding the Parent-Endorsed Lockup (`5.3.3`), and omit unused lines, unofficial abbreviations, personal numbers, quotes, hype, altered endorsements, or unauthorized badges (`5.3.4`). | Email client & copy check |
| `E04-C4` | Shared correspondence and slide templates are registered as `TPL-E04` in `Library-Index.md` and Section 7 pinned to `E04 (vMAJOR.MINOR)` (`5.4.1`), and company descriptions reproduce approved `C01 §5.5.1` boilerplates verbatim (`5.4.2`). | Template & boilerplate audit |
| `E04-C5` | Operational workbooks include a leading `Overview` tab with required metadata and `A03` attribution (`5.5.1`), apply `B03` typography, frozen headers, and `WCAG` 2.2 Level AA `B02` styling (`5.5.2`), enforce `C04` units, `YYYY-MM-DD` dates, non-blank cells, and `C02` status styling (`5.5.3`), and register shared workbook templates as `TPL-E04` (`5.5.4`). | Workbook & template audit |

Exceptions to any requirement in this standard require a formal, time-boxed waiver approved and logged in accordance with `A04`.
