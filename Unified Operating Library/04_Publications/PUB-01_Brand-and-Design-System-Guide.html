01 / Brand foundation

Architecture, voice, and operating principles

Irvantri Standards operates a Branded House architecture (A03) grounded in objective, verifiable language (C01) and controlled terminology (C02).

A03 · C01 · C02 · D03
01

Integrity

Display active C02 lifecycle states explicitly without prohibited synonyms, and render domain record flags (incomplete, exempt, locked) distinct from lifecycle badges (D03 §5.5.1).

02

Precision

Use uppercase library codes, exact International Organization for Standardization (ISO) 8601 dates, and unambiguous units (A02, C04).

03

Reliability

Apply deterministic single-source tokens, consistent component states, and explicit user confirmations (D01, D03, D05).

04

Progress

Prioritize open reviews, active assignments, and required operational decisions ahead of decorative charts (D03 §5.5.2).

Approved short corporate description (C01 §5.5.1)

Authoritative company boilerplate

Irvantri Standards (Irvantri Standards Pte. Ltd.) builds practical operating standards, governance frameworks, and structured digital and documentation systems. Focused on clarity, traceability, and repeatable execution, Irvantri Standards helps organizations replace ad-hoc work with durable, auditable baselines. A member of Irvantri Group.

Operational interface discipline (D03 §5.5, B04 §5.3.4)

Seven operating principles

  1. Trust through restraintRely on calibrated neutral surfaces, structured 4 px grids, measured typographic hierarchy, and zero ornamental noise.
  2. Precision over ornamentPair clear labels with traceable identifiers, explicit ownership, and non-color provenance callouts.
  3. Action before vanity analyticsLead operational dashboards with open reviews, active assignments, and required user decisions.
  4. Honest system statesUse exact C02 controlled status strings and never hide exceptions, waivers, or locked states.
  5. Traceable operationsRequire explicit user actions and confirmation dialogs for consequential exports and destructive changes.
  6. Adapt without fragmentationMaintain identical status semantics, keyboard operability, and reading order from 320 px through 1440 px.
  7. No decorative takeoverExclude orbital rings, wave textures, and unapproved gradients from dense work surfaces, forms, and tables.

CLEAR SPACE · B01 §5.3.1

0.25× unobstructed margin

Maintain at least 0.25× the Primary Symbol width (w) of unobstructed Clear Space on all four sides.

CO-BRANDING · B01 §5.5

Tier 4 optical parity

Size the Irvantri Standards mark at Optical Parity with partner marks, separated by a 1 px vertical rule centered inside a minimum 1.0× symbol-width horizontal gap. Publication requires a signed Approver agreement and rights-holder permission.

PROHIBITED MISUSE · B01 §5.6.1

Zero alteration permitted

Do not stretch, skew, rotate, recolor, apply gradients or shadows, rearrange lockup typography, crop, enclose in unapproved frames, or redraw symbols outside registered AST-B01 masters. The App Icon Tile is the one approved frame (B01 §5.6.2).

SMALL-FORMAT SYMBOL · B01 §5.3.4

16 px to below 28 px

Browser tabs, bookmarks, and 16 px system icons use AST-B01-17 or AST-B01-18 (B05 §5.1.4). At 28 px and larger, use the Primary Symbol. Never print the Small-Format Symbol.

FAVICON · B01 §5.1.4, §5.4.4

SVG first, ICO fallback

Link AST-B01-17 as the SVG favicon; it switches to Reversed White when prefers-color-scheme: dark applies. AST-B01-19_Favicon.ico holds lossless 16, 32, and 48 px frames and stays full color.

APP ICON TILE · B01 §5.6.2

Symbol at two thirds of the tile

A Pure White tile carries the full-color symbol, giving 0.25× Clear Space on all four sides. Use AST-B01-21 (180 px Apple touch icon) and AST-B01-22 / AST-B01-23 (192 px and 512 px web app manifest icons).

Signal Red restriction and contrast thresholds (B02 §5.3.2, §5.5.1)

Never apply raw Signal Red (#DE0029) to body copy, form validation messages, or error text on light neutral surfaces. Error and danger text uses Danger (#A42332). Normal text requires at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio; large text and non-text UI boundaries require at least 3.0:1.

Responsive breakpoints align with D02 (v2.0.1), accessibility controls align with WCAG 2.2 Level AA and Accessible Rich Internet Applications (ARIA) rules in D04 (v1.2.1), and generated outputs enforce D05 (v1.1.1).

ACCESSIBILITY · D04 (v1.2.1)

WCAG 2.2 Level AA checklist

  • Use semantic landmarks (header, nav, main, footer) and one page-level h1.
  • Render keyboard focus indicators as a 3 px solid #074C96 outline with 3 px offset (D04 §5.2.4).
  • Maintain 44 px × 44 px targets with at least 12 px separation on Compact viewports (D04 §5.1.3).
  • Announce dynamic status updates via polite ARIA Live Regions without stealing focus (D04 §5.3.2).
  • Honor prefers-reduced-motion and avoid color-only signaling (D04 §5.1.2, §5.3.3).
EXPORTS & GOVERNANCE · D05 (v1.1.1)

Traceable generated outputs

  • Store Audit Timestamps in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) using ISO 8601; display local time zones only with explicit offsets (D05 §5.1.3).
  • Never auto-generate finalized formal reports on routine save actions; require explicit user confirmation (D05 §5.2.5).
  • Mark unfinalized exports with controlled Draft or In Review labels rather than informal provisional wording (D05 §5.2.6).
  • Preserve zero blank table cells (None or —) and declared International System of Units (SI) symbols (C04 §5.3.1, §5.4.1).
  • Never mix tenant branding and Irvantri Group parent branding within the same interface view (D02 §5.2.4).
