## 4. Brand Foundations & Operating Principles (`PUB-01`, `B01`–`B04`, `C01`, `D03`)

Synthesized from the **Brand and Design System Guide** (`PUB-01`) and active Series `B` through Series `D` standards, Irvantri Standards anchors its work in four brand pillars and seven operating principles.

### 4.1 Four Brand Pillars (`PUB-01`, `D03 §5.5`)

**Table 5.** Four Brand Pillars (`PUB-01`, `D03 §5.5`)

| Pillar | Operational Definition | Governing Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01 · Integrity** | Display active `C02` lifecycle states explicitly without prohibited synonyms, and render domain record flags (incomplete, exempt, locked) distinct from lifecycle badges. | `D03 §5.5.1`, `C02 §5.4.1` |
| **02 · Precision** | Use uppercase American Standard Code for Information Interchange (`ASCII`) library codes, exact International Organization for Standardization (`ISO`) 8601 dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`), and unambiguous units. | `A02 §5.2.1`, `C04 §5.1.1` |
| **03 · Reliability** | Apply deterministic single-source tokens (`AST-D01-01`), consistent component states, and explicit user confirmations for consequential operations. | `D01 §5.1`, `D03 §5.4.1`, `D05 §5.2.5` |
| **04 · Progress** | Prioritize open reviews, active assignments, and required operational decisions ahead of decorative charts or vanity analytics. | `D03 §5.5.2` |

### 4.2 Seven Operating Principles (`PUB-01`, `D03 §5.5`, `B04 §5.3.4`)

1. **Trust through restraint:** Rely on calibrated neutral surfaces, structured `4 px` grids, measured typographic hierarchy, and zero ornamental noise.
2. **Precision over ornament:** Pair clear labels with traceable identifiers, explicit ownership, and non-color provenance callouts.
3. **Action before vanity analytics:** Lead operational dashboards with open reviews, active assignments, and required user decisions.
4. **Honest system states:** Use exact `C02` controlled status strings and never hide exceptions, waivers, or locked states.
5. **Traceable operations:** Require explicit user actions and confirmation dialogs for consequential exports and destructive changes.
6. **Adapt without fragmentation:** Maintain identical status semantics, keyboard operability, and reading order from `320 px` through `1440 px`.
7. **No decorative takeover:** Exclude orbital rings, wave textures, and unapproved gradients from dense work surfaces, forms, and tables.

### 4.3 Visual & Editorial System Summary (`B01`–`B04`, `C01`, `E01`, `PUB-01`)

* **Primary Brand Palette (`B02 §5.1.1`):** Core brand colors are Brand Blue (`#074C96`), Signal Red (`#DE0029`), Deep Navy (`#092B55`), and Royal Navy (`#102F60`). These pair with a calibrated neutral scale and five-state semantic colors meeting Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (`WCAG`) 2.2 Level AA contrast (`B02 §5.3.1`, `§5.5.1`).
* **Marks & Signature Graphic Device (`B01 §5.3`, `B04 §5.3.1`):** Formal covers may display a `24%` Signal Red (`#DE0029`) and `76%` Deep Navy (`#092B55`) split accent bar in horizontal or `4 mm` vertical left-spine orientation (`B04 (v1.2.0) §5.3.1`, `E01 §5.1.1`). Official marks preserve `0.25×` Primary Symbol width Clear Space (`B01 §5.3.1`) and `260 px` digital / `64 mm` print Parent-Endorsed Lockup minimums (`B01 (v2.1.0) §5.3.2`). Browser tabs and other renders from `16 px` to below `28 px` use the registered Small-Format Symbol, and app icons use the Pure White App Icon Tile (`B01 (v2.1.0) §5.3.4`, `§5.6.2`).
* **Brand Typography (`B03 (v2.0.0) §5.1.1`–`§5.1.3`):** The unified `SIL OFL-1.1` Noto Sans superfamily governs all controlled deliverables. `Noto Sans Display` serves display headings and `Noto Sans` serves body prose and tables. `Noto Sans Mono` (with `Liberation Mono`, `DejaVu Sans Mono`, and system monospace fallbacks) formats version identifiers, codes, and file paths.
* **Editorial Posture (`C01 §5.1.1`–`§5.1.2`, `§5.6.1`):** Communications use objective, matter-of-fact United States English (`en-US`) grounded in verifiable facts and observable outcomes. Unsubstantiated superlatives and promotional hyperbole are excluded.
